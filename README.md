# Scape & Run: The Last Decade

A Hearts of Iron IV total conversion mod based on the world and parasite concepts of **Scape and Run: Parasites** by Dhanantry, expanded into an original 2018-2030 alternate-history apocalypse setting.

This is a **PUBLIC ALPHA** for the years 2018-2023 (roughly). 2023 is the cutoff before the main events and interesting stuff happens, I'm sorry about that.
**Please provide feedback** through my discord: honaaaa. Or the github issues page (i guess)
**It will be rough around the edges** it is in active development and unpolished. Missing textures, weird mechanics or bugs are expected.
**Everything is subject to change** every arc every focus every story-line may change in the future as I have rewritten entire focus lines because I wasn't intensely happy with them.

This is not the development repo, this only serves to publicize versions. The actual development repo is a private one. If you want to contribute consistently please DM me.

## Current Scope

The repository currently targets **Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.\*** and contains the foundations of a full conversion, including:

- A custom 2018/2020 bookmark (2020 may be retired and is currently not really implemented due to pacing) for the Last Decade scenario.
- Reworked country setup, tags, histories, and state files.
- Playable and scripted factions including the European Federation, United States, Japan, the Iris, Knox, South America, Oceania, China, Africa, and Sweramoa.
- Custom technology areas for modern warfare, aerospace, industry, electronics, parasite response, and parasite development.
- Parasite units, equipment, buildings, infestation systems, decisions, events, and dynamic modifiers.
- H&A/European Federation systems, including balance of power, military industrial organizations, hyperbiology content, and orbital program work.
- Custom resources such as fissile material and parasite biomass.
- Focus trees, localisation, icons, interface edits, and loading screen work in active development.

Some of this is implemented, some of it is rough.

## Core Factions

**European Federation / H&A**  
The main human anchor of this world. The European Federation begins as a hegemonic modern power, but the decade steadily reveals that H&A, the industrial conglomerate underneath much of civilization, may be the more functional institution when the world starts failing.

**United States**  
Still powerful, still armed, and still shaped by its rivalry with Europe.

**The Iris**  
The source of the parasite invasion. It observes first, adapts second, and escalates only after humanity has shown it what to counter.

**Knox**  
A local exception inside the parasite crisis. Not quite human, not Iris-aligned, and not safely understood.

**Japan, China, South America, Oceania, Africa, and Sweramoa**  
Regional powers and survival projects caught between old geopolitics and a biological war that does not care about borders except as useful infrastructure.
